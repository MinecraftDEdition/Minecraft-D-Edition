Privacy

Account data

Minecraft: D Edition stores the email address supplied by the identity provider, a public account identifier, your chosen username, and any skin you upload. Passwords are handled by the identity provider and are never sent to or stored by Minecraft: D Edition.

Session cookies are required to keep you signed in and protect profile changes. They are HTTP-only, secure, and expire automatically.

This project does not sell account information. You may deactivate your profile without removing it, reactivate it after signing in, or permanently delete the MCDE profile and its linked Auth0 identity from the account dashboard. A self-service data export will be added before accounts are required by a public game release.

For account or privacy help, contact the project owner through the official GitHub repository.

Minecraft: D Edition is a fan-made project and is not affiliated with Mojang Studios or Microsoft.
